Our Wedding Halls

Oasis Lawn

The Oasis Lawn is our premium wedding space, designed to host grand ceremonies and receptions. With luxurious interiors, sparkling chandeliers, and a spacious seating arrangement, it creates the perfect atmosphere for unforgettable celebrations.

  • Seating Capacity: 700 – 1500 guests (with extra 500 floating)
  • Stage Setup: Spacious central stage with backdrop customization options.
  • Seating Style Options:
    • Cluster Seating: Perfect for cocktail evenings or casual gatherings.
    • Theatre Style: Continuous rows, suitable for sangeet or cultural functions.
    • Dining Arrangement: Large dining area attached, with buffet counters around the periphery.
  • Parking: Valet parking available with large capacity.
    • 📍Address: Jumail's West, Santacruz, West Mumbai-41039
    💰Price:8,00,000

Garden Fate

The Garden Fate Lawn is a spacious open-air venue surrounded by lush greenery and elegant landscaping, making it the perfect choice for weddings, receptions, and grand celebrations. With decorative lighting and ample seating capacity, it provides a refreshing outdoor ambiance.

  • Seating Capacity: 1000 – 2500 guests (banquet style)
  • Floating Capacity: Up to 700 guests
  • Seating Style Options:
    • Round table seating with 10-15 chairs each
    • Open-air lawn seating with sofa lounges & cocktail tables
    • Stage setup with guest chairs arranged in rows for ceremonies
  • Extra Add-ons: Buffet counters along the garden edges for smooth movement.
    • 📍Address:Indrayni Nagar, Behind Zey Studio,Ahmednagar-42392
    💰Price:4,00,000

Melody Lawn

The Melody Lawn is a vibrant open-air venue, especially popular for Mehndi and Haldi ceremonies. Known for its lively pink-themed décor, this lawn creates the perfect festive atmosphere filled with joy, color, and celebration.

  • Seating Capacity: 1500 – 2500 guests
  • Speciality: Haldi & Mehendi (Pink Themed)
  • Seating Style Options:
    • Vibrant floor seating with cushions for Mehndi/Haldi functions
    • Pink & yellow themed canopy with low tables and stools
    • Banquet round tables for evening dinners (optional)
  • Extra Add-ons: Special mehendi stage for the bride/groom with floral décor.
    • 📍Address: Nyati Road, Behind Dhanshree,Pune-412308
    💰Price:6,00,000
